FrankNet

Ongoing · networks · ai · operations

Overview

FrankNet is a personal systems laboratory focused on the interaction between network infrastructure, AI orchestration, and operational security. The environment blends physical hardware, virtualized networks, and experimental automation.

Core Areas

  • Network topology & segmentation
  • AI-assisted operations
  • Security monitoring & logging
  • Failure testing & recovery

Architecture

The lab is designed as a modular environment, allowing components to be replaced or isolated without destabilizing the whole system.

Status

Actively evolving. Documentation is updated incrementally as experiments are completed.

Korum WorldView

2025–present · geospatial intelligence · signal correlation · real-time ops

Focus

Korum WorldView is a real-time geospatial signal intelligence layer that feeds upstream into the KorumOS Neural Council. It correlates live data streams across aircraft, satellites, ships, weather, cyber activity, GPS jamming, internet health, and news — then escalates compressed anomaly packets to the Council when correlated signals cross severity thresholds.

Signal Sources

  • Aircraft tracking — FlightRadar24 / OpenSky
  • Satellite positions — N2YO TLE orbital data
  • Maritime AIS — AISHub ship movements
  • Cyber threat feeds — AlienVault OTX, AbuseIPDB
  • Weather — Windy API
  • GPS jamming, internet health, news, social signals

Severity Gate

  • WATCH — Alert panel only. No AI action required.
  • ALERT — Alert panel + manual Council trigger enabled.
  • CRITICAL — Auto-escalates to KorumOS Council with compressed context packet.

Architecture

Correlation runs on H3 hexagonal grid cells (~1,770 km²) with a 30-minute sliding window. Escalation packets are capped at ~250 tokens — enough for the Council to understand the situation without requiring the full raw event payload.

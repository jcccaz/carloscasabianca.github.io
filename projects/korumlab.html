Korum Lab

2025–present · pre-analysis · adversarial testing · query refinement

Focus

Korum Lab is an experimental pre-processing environment for refining decisions before they enter the KorumOS Neural Council. Raw, unstructured text is extracted into the Korum Ontology, attacked by a local adversarial model, and arbitrated by a Governor — producing a structured, pre-tested brief that the full Council can act on immediately.

Pipeline

  • Step 1 — Extract — GPT-4o forces raw text into the Korum Ontology: evidence, assumptions, unknowns, risks, recommendation.
  • Step 2 — Attack — Local Mistral 7B Red Teams the extracted decision. No alignment filters. No hedging.
  • Step 3 — Arbitrate — GPT-4o Governor resolves the attack. Issues GO / NO-GO / CONDITIONAL with confidence score, failure triggers, and monitoring requirements.
  • Step 4 — Push to KorumOS — The pre-tested brief is packaged and submitted to the KorumOS Neural Council for full governed execution planning.

Design Principle

The Council should inherit pre-work, not repeat it. By the time a query reaches KorumOS from Korum Lab, the surface-level risks are already surfaced and the Governor has already ruled. The Council focuses on what's next — not what's obvious.

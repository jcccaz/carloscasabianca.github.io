KorumOS

2025–present · decision governance · enterprise AI · adversarial systems

Focus

KorumOS is an adversarial decision governance platform for high-stakes operations. It wraps multiple AI providers in a zero-trust governance stack — forcing models to debate, challenge, and defend outputs before a decision is issued. Every output is scored, sourced, and recorded. Nothing ships as a recommendation unless it survives adversarial pressure.

Core Layers

  • Korum Shield — 3-pass PII redaction. Models never see raw identifiers.
  • Neural Council — Multi-model sequential reasoning. Builder, Challenger, Finalizer, Red Team.
  • Confidence Governor — Calibrated fact and decision scoring with hard floors and caps.
  • Decision Ledger — Tamper-evident, hash-chained audit trail of every council event.

Current Applications

  • Telecom disaster recovery coordination — tested against Sandy-scale scenarios
  • Enterprise RFP / proposal intelligence with win likelihood scoring
  • Decision Risk Exposure engine — quantifies undefended decisions in dollar terms
  • Multi-domain council workflows: Legal, Finance, Healthcare, Cyber, War Room

Design Principle

If a user would not act on the output with confidence, it does not ship. The system produces decisions, not answers.

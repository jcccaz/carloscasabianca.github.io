AI Operations Research

2024–2025 · systems · ai · operations

Focus

This project examines how AI systems behave under prolonged operational pressure, ambiguous input, and repeated evaluation. The goal is to bridge the gap between theoretical alignment and operational reality.

Key Questions

  • How does consistency degrade over time?
  • What signals indicate internal state degradation?
  • Where do current evaluation methods fail in edge cases?

Outputs

The project produces a series of papers, internal-style memos, and experimental frameworks designed for stress-testing large language models in non-standard environments.
